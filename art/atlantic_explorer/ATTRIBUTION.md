## Changes in this repository

The source FBX was imported into Blender, given deterministic glTF PBR materials, triangulated, transform-normalized, and exported as GLB. The two 4096 px color maps were limited to 2048 px for VR use. Viewports, lights, and eight thrusters were separated into named runtime meshes. XR/failure anchors and simplified collision proxies were added. No claim is made that the fictional vehicle is pressure-rated or engineering-accurate.
