# Technical data audit

Reviewed 17 November 2026 against the downloaded TG 22.3 report/enclosures and Report 2G-9C. This audit records evidence and recommended corrections; it does not change simulation tuning or certify the boat's exact arrangement. References below use the printed section/page labels in the hosted transcription. Some sections repeat page 1 for the summary and first detailed page.

## Glossary status review

All 37 German term records were checked for their status implications. Leaving most terms `estimated` is correct: English technical prose confirms systems but not the exact German label on U-505. Do not promote the entire glossary on the strength of an English report. The bibliography confirms the word `Tiefenmesser`, and references `Tauchzellen...Entlüftungsanlage`; that does not authenticate every game label or compound.

Concrete English-body corrections are needed for `Bugtorpedoraum` (avoid unsourced majority-sleeping claim), `Unteroffizierraum` (game zone includes chiefs), `Lenzverteiler` (suction manifold to pumps rather than water dumped into one bilge), `Kommandant` (a curtained stateroom is still a cabin), and `Kriegstagebuch` (separate archive document, not TG enclosure). `Kühlwasser`'s seawater cooling is supported by S48-5; general descriptions of hull valves, pumps, vent/blow physics and port/starboard can remain explanations with label status estimated. Actual control shape and placement for `Abstellhebel`, `Anblaseventil`, `Lenzventil`, and the placeholder charge terms remain unverified.

## Generated assumptions list

`assumptionsHtml()` is a manual selection of records, not a traversal of every assumed value. It omits seven `data-items.items[*].status.weight = assumed` records and the assumed11:40 timeline event, and it does not expose modeled strainer Cd/position because that parent is labeled real. Door/drain assumptions live in prose and have no per-record status for a collector to discover. Hull dimensions/compartment estimates are also omitted.

Recommended correction: add status metadata to modeled door/drain/geometry fields; collect records recursively, including field-valued status objects and `*_status` siblings; retain labels distinguishing estimated, assumed, and placeholder. Cover parent-scoped assumptions such as hydrostatics/initial water without listing every scalar separately. Update the explanatory codex claim to match what is actually collected, and do not silently promote a whole mixed-status record because one fact was sourced.

No Skizzenbuch plates were consulted. Exact capture-day pump availability, charge deployment, scuttling lineup, strainer bore, item placement, all numerical water states and the game's detailed hydrostatics remain unresolved.
